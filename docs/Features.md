# ExamVerse AI - Key Features

ExamVerse AI is built with features specifically targeting Indian competitive exam patterns.

## 1. AI Tutor & Doubt-Solving
- **24/7 Question Answering**: Instant solutions to queries across all subjects (History, Quantitative Aptitude, Physics, etc.).
- **Step-by-Step Derivations**: Explanations for math/science problems rather than just raw answers.
- **Multilingual Explanations**: Ability to explain concepts in Hinglish, Hindi, and English.

## 2. Dynamic Mock Tests
- **Real Exam Simulator**: Full-length test interface mimicking UPSC, JEE, and SSC portals.
- **Syllabus-Linked Tagging**: Every question in the database is tagged with its syllabus micro-topic.
- **Immediate Analytics**: Shows time spent per question, accuracy metrics, and passing threshold comparison.

## 3. Previous Year Questions (PYQs)
- **Topic-wise PYQ Search**: Search past papers by topic (e.g., UPSC PYQs on Indus Valley Civilization).
- **AI Answer Keys**: Detailed analysis of why options are correct or incorrect.

## 4. AI Study Planner
- **Adaptive Calendar**: Auto-adjusts study schedules if a student misses a milestone.
- **Daily Goals**: Breaks down large syllabi into bite-sized daily tasks.

## 5. Revision & Voice Assistant
- **AI Short Notes**: Converts long articles or book chapters into structured revision summaries.
- **Voice Quiz**: Interact with the platform hands-free during breaks or commutes to revise current affairs.
