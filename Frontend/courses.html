3. Exam Overview (Description List)

Understand the core scope of each course category:

UPSC (Union Public Service Commission)
Prepares aspirants for administrative services (IAS, IPS, IFS) with general studies, optionals, and essay writing support.
SSC (Staff Selection Commission)
Targets government staff appointments through exams like SSC CGL and CHSL covering quant, English, and general reasoning.
Banking
Specialized study sets for SBI, IBPS, and RBI Officer recruitments, prioritizing high-speed numerical and logical aptitude.
Railways
Comprehensive study tracks for non-technical popular categories (NTPC) and junior engineer job profiles.
JEE (Joint Entrance Examination)
Covers intensive PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) coaching models for admissions to IITs, NITs, and IIITs.
NEET (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test)
Focuses on advanced Biology, Physics, and Chemistry practice sheets for medical undergraduate admissions in India.
